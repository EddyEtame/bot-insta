# BC DESIGN — Visual Direction & Artistic System

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### The Three Tests Every BC Design Must Pass

**The Billboard Test:**
Would this stop traffic at 80km/h on a Toulouse highway? If the message isn't readable at 2 seconds and 50 meters — it fails.

**The Scroll Test:**
Would someone stop scrolling on Instagram for this? If it looks like any other gym ad — it fails.

**The Permission Test (Axe 2 and 4):**
Would a woman who has never boxed feel this is for her? If it looks intimidating, aggressive, or exclusive — it fails.

**Global palette:**
```
Navy Logo:  #20254B  → Official logo fill (BOXING and CENTER both)
Bronze:     #6D3111  → Logo contour accent (logo file only)
White:      #F5F5F5  → Primary text

Orange:     #FF7A00  → Axe 2 badge only
```

**LOGO RULE (overrides everything):** the logo is a FILE, not text.
Place BC_Logo_Officiel_Transparent.png (dark bg) or _FondBlanc.png (light bg).
Never recompose "BOXING CENTER" in type. Never recolor. The full rules
live in BC_Brand_Guidelines.pdf — the canonical brand document.

**The Color Economy Rule — one dominant energy per axis:**
- Axe 1: RED is the hero (89€ only). Everything else white or gray. No exceptions.
- Axe 2: GOLD/WHITE light (empowerment, not aggression)
- Axe 3: COLD BLUE + red dots (urban, territorial, network)
- Axe 4: Each panel has its own world. Panel 6 = solid red. The answer.

## TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

```
Display/Impact:  Bebas Neue or Barlow Condensed
                 → Headlines, prices, dominant text
                 → All caps, tight letter-spacing
                 → Forward italic (5-8°) for momentum

Body:            Montserrat SemiBold → Subtitles
                 Montserrat Regular → Body, disciplines

Labels:          IBM Plex Mono → Technical labels, codes
```

## CINEMATIC TREATMENTS BY AXE

### Axe 1 — Price Hero Treatment
- Background: Real BC gym photo (ring, flags, murals)
- Darken to 50% original brightness
- Spotlight from above center onto the ring
- Heavy vignette all edges → corners near-black
- Split-tone: warm amber in midtones, cold blue-black in shadows
- Selective color: ONLY red elements keep full saturation (ring ropes)
- Film grain: 6-8% opacity

**The 89€ Treatment:**
- Ultra-bold condensed, maximum size (70% of poster width)
- Color: #E8001C
- Outer glow: red aura, 30-40px radius, 35% opacity
- Drop shadow: black, offset 6px down / 4px right, 50% opacity, 15px blur
- Crack texture inside letterforms: 15% opacity
- Slight forward italic (5°) suggesting aggression and momentum

**The 150€ Treatment:**
- 30% white opacity (ghost of a defeated price)
- Blade slash: thick diagonal, tapers to points at tips (not a line — a cut)
- Horizontal motion blur 3px on the text only (it's already leaving)

### Axe 2 — Championship Light Treatment
- Golden spotlight from directly above, illuminating the people in center
- Everything else falls to deep blue-black shadow
- Skin tones stay warm amber
- Red ring ropes stay vivid
- Dark gradient bottom 45% → 90% black (text zone)

**Headline Treatment:**
- Pure white letters with GOLDEN outer glow (#FFB347, 35px, 40%)
- Subtle white inner glow (blinding core)
- Warm drop shadow (golden brown, 6px, 20px blur)
- The period on "PEUT BOXER." is as large as a bullet — final, definitive

### Axe 3 — Urban Network Treatment
- Cold blue-black color grade overall
- Ring ropes catch light and glow slightly blue
- Maximum contrast: blacks to pure black
- Network overlay: 6 red glowing dots with connecting lines (1px, #4DAAFF, 40%)
- Each dot: 15px red glow radius
- Atmospheric fog at base of ring: cold blue-white, 20% opacity

**Headline Treatment:**
- Chrome/metallic texture in letterforms (20% opacity — feels like steel)
- Cold blue electric glow (#4DAAFF, 35px, 40%)
- Strong drop shadow: pure black, 8px, 25px blur

### Axe 4 — Multi-World Treatment (6 panels)
- Panel 1 (Boxe Anglaise): Near B&W, ONLY red gloves at full color. Spotlight from above.
- Panel 2 (Boxing Lady): Warm golden spotlight on women in center. Deep blue-black shadow.
- Panel 3 (MMA): Deep red cast. Motion blur on gloves ONLY (3px). Raw and immediate.
- Panel 4 (Crosstraining): Red walls at maximum saturation. Room feels on fire.
- Panel 5 (Boxe Éducative): Child under warm championship gold spotlight. Heroic, not cute.
- Panel 6 (The Decision): Solid red #E8001C. Cracked earth 89€. The answer.

## PRICE BADGE — AXE 2 SPECIFICATION

**Shape:** Clean parallelogram, 8° tilt. NO ornamental borders. NO decorative edges.
**Background:** Deep red #C8001A with subtle gradient (darker top, lighter bottom)
**Edge:** 2px bright red #E8001C only — like a credit card edge
**Drop shadow:** Black, 8px down, 25px blur — floats above poster

**Internal hierarchy:**
1. "89€" — white ultra-bold, 96pt — dominant
2. "AU LIEU DE 150 €" — white strikethrough, 70% opacity, 24pt
3. "3 MOIS ILLIMITÉS · 6 SALLES" — white bold, 20pt
