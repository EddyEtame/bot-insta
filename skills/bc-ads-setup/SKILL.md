# BC ADS SETUP — Google Ads · Meta Ads · CRM

## SECTION 1 — CAMPAIGN FRAME (été 2026)

Offer: 3 mois illimités 6 salles — 89 € au lieu de 150 € · live 12/06 → 26/06 · mandatory mention everywhere: "Planning aménagé fin juillet–mi-août". Landing: boxingcenter.fr/offre-ete (Systeme.io or WordPress+Elementor).
Budget split (boss-validated): 50% Axe 1 Prix Choc · 35% Axe 2 Décision d'Été · 15% Axe 3 Pass Toulouse (+ Axe 4 organic complement).

## SECTION 2 — GOOGLE ADS (Search RSA)

- Structure: 1 campaign Search → ad groups by intent: "boxe toulouse" / "salle de sport + ville" / "mma toulouse" / "boxing fitness femme".
- Keywords: salle de boxe toulouse · mma toulouse · salle de sport portet/balma/ramonville/saint-cyprien · boxe thai toulouse · boxing fitness · cross training toulouse.
- RSA: 8 headlines ≤30 chars, 3 descriptions ≤90 chars (use the validated set from campaign history; price headline carries "89 € au lieu de 150 €"). Display path: boxingcenter.fr/offre-ete.
- Geo: 20 km radius around each of the 6 gyms — BALMA · SAINT-CYPRIEN · ÉTATS-UNIS · MINIMES · RAMONVILLE · PORTET.
- Budget scenarios: 500 € test · **1 000–1 500 € recommended** · 2 000 €+ aggressive. Validate with the boss before launch.

## SECTION 3 — META ADS

- 3 validated copy variants: vente directe (Axe 1 visual) · nouveau public (Axe 2, Permission Test applies) · retargeting (urgence 26 juin).
- Prerequisites: Pixel installed on WordPress → audiences: site visitors 180 d, IG/FB engagers, lookalike adhérents · load only boss-validated visuals.
- Red rule: red belongs to the 89 € only; CTA button allowed on digital versions.

## SECTION 4 — CRM FIRST (cheapest, fastest — send BEFORE ads go live)

Order of fire: 1) SMS 160 chars (versions with/without emoji ready) → 2) WhatsApp rich message → 3) Email (subject + body ready). Existing member base = warmest audience; this protects ad budget.

## SECTION 5 — KPIs & DAILY ROUTINE (12/06 → 26/06)

- Target cost/lead: **7–12 €**. North-star: leads → essais → inscriptions 89 €.
- Daily check: spend, CPL, CTR (<0.8% Search or <1% Meta CTR → rotate creative), search terms (add negatives), pause any ad group with CPL >18 € after 3 days.
- Weekly: shift budget toward the best axe; report to the boss in 5 lines max.

## SECTION 6 — TECHNICAL ACCESS CHECKLIST (verify before launch)

OVH (DNS) · WordPress admin · Meta Business Manager + Pixel · Google Ads account + billing · Google Business Profile (6 fiches) · boutique en ligne. Any missing access = blocker to flag to the boss immediately.

## SECTION 7 — OFFER HYGIENE

Été 89 € and Rentrée Portet 295 € are separate campaigns, separate creatives, separate landing pages. Never one ad carrying both.
