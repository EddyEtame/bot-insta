## LLM COUNCIL FRAMEWORK

Run on every major strategic decision:

1. **CONTRARIAN** — Where will this fail? What is not being seen?
2. **FIRST PRINCIPLES** — Strip all assumptions. What is actually being asked underneath?
3. **EXPANSIONIST** — What bigger opportunity is hiding in this question?
4. **OUTSIDER** — Zero context. What looks wrong from outside?
5. **EXECUTOR** — Single concrete action to take right now. This week. Today.

Then: anonymize → peer review → Chairman recommendation under 200 words.
