# BC VIDEO PRODUCTION — 100% AI Video Pipeline

## SECTION 1 — PIPELINE (validated June 2026)

| Stage | Primary tool | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Scene generation | Grok Imagine via SuperGrok (already paid) — 720p, 15 s std / 30 s max, native audio, Extend +2–10 s | Kling 3.0 (best value) · Runway Gen-4.5 (camera motion) |
| Hero shots (1080p) | Veo 3.1 via Gemini — best realism + native audio, limited free quota: spend it ONLY on hero scenes | — |
| French voiceover | ElevenLabs v3 (emotion tags) — most natural FR; free tier 10k chars/month | Canva AI voice · Veo native voice |
| Editing & export | Canva Pro: assembly, dynamic subtitles, licensed percussive music, official logo open/close, final text screen | CapCut |

**Escalation rule (inherited):** 3 failed generations on one scene → switch tool. Never fight a model.

## SECTION 2 — INTER-SCENE CONSISTENCY (the #1 failure of AI ads)

Paste this block at the top of EVERY scene prompt:

```
CONSISTENT WORLD — same modern combat-sports gym interior across all scenes:
warm/dark cinematic grade, deep navy shadows (#0C1829), warm amber practical
lights, international flags hanging from the ceiling, red and blue mats,
red ring ropes. Same lighting style every scene. No text on screen.
No logos. 16:9, smooth cinematic camera. Photoreal, not cartoon.
```

Rules: generate all scenes the same day with the same settings · never let the model render text or logos (text is added in Canva, logo = official file only) · clip length 8–12 s per scene · one VO audio track per scene (separate files) so any scene stays cuttable.

## SECTION 3 — DETACHABLE SCENE PROTOCOL

When the boss says a mention "peut bouger" (e.g. White Net partnership):
1. The scene is fully autonomous — no visual or VO cross-reference to other scenes.
2. Its VO is a separate audio file.
3. The edit places it between two hard cuts → removable/replaceable in one click.
4. The final text screen keeps the mention on its own line → deletable without relayout.

## SECTION 4 — VOICEOVER RULES (French)

- Voice: FR masculine, energetic, sports-trailer tone. ElevenLabs v3 with emotion ramp (build-up → apex on final scene).
- Text = the boss's script EXACTLY. Zero paraphrase. Check: "salles" spelling, no English words.
- Subtitles: Bebas Neue / Montserrat, white with black edge, dynamic word-by-word.

## SECTION 5 — OFFER HYGIENE (critical)

One video = one offer. NEVER mix:
- Offre ÉTÉ: 89 € / 3 mois / 6 salles / until 26 juin / "Planning aménagé fin juillet–mi-août"
- Offre RENTRÉE (Portet only): 295 € l'année / 3× sans frais
A video carrying 295 € must contain zero trace of 89 €, and vice versa.

## SECTION 6 — EXPORTS

16:9 master (YouTube, site, écrans salle) · 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Stories) · square 1:1 if Meta feed needed. Logo = `BC_Logo_Officiel_Transparent.png` opening (1.5 s) + closing. Final screen built as Canva text, never AI-generated.

## SECTION 7 — QUALITY GATE BEFORE SENDING TO THE BOSS

1. Watch with sound OFF: does the story read in images alone?
2. Watch with sound ON: VO/image sync, no scene contradicting reality (address, colors, equipment).
3. Scroll Test on the 9:16 cut: do the first 2 seconds stop the thumb?
4. Offer check: right offer, right price, right legal mention, zero spelling errors (frame-by-frame on text screens).
