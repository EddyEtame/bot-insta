# Roblox Watch

Eddy's standing order: **"RUN TECHNOLOGICAL WATCHES AND DO RESEARCH ONLINE!"** Designing from imagination when references exist is the failure he calls producing low quality with too many references available. Research is not preparation for the work; it IS work he pays for, and he can tell from the specificity of your output whether it happened.

## Sources, in order

1. **The studio's own canon first.** Constitution, Design Constitution, Handbook, Project Bible, decision logs, the Horror Castle archive (`discord/docs/` — Choir spec, Echo Hall spec, ui-flow, figma prompts), the Master Bible. Eddy plants context in folders and expects you to find it — sibling folders count. Never ask what the docs already answer.
2. **The genre's summit.** For Project 001: **DOORS** (the archetype — door-loop curiosity engine, entity telegraphs, Guiding Light teaching system), **Pressure** (DOORS-like done differently — searchable rooms, harsher tone, ensemble entity cast), **Rooms** (the minimalist ancestor — dread through austerity), **The Mimic** (linear story horror chapters), **Apeirophobia** (backrooms exploration), plus whatever currently tops Roblox horror charts — verify with a live search, the front page shifts monthly.
3. **The wider craft.** Non-Roblox horror for mechanisms (Amnesia's sanity pressure, Alien: Isolation's unscripted stalker, P.T.'s looping corridor literacy, Iron Lung's dread-through-instruments), award-tier UI/motion for menus and interfaces, GDC talks on horror pacing and game feel.
4. **Roblox platform truth.** DevForum + creator docs for engine capabilities and current-year changes (audio API, lighting tech, StreamingEnabled behavior, Byfron/anti-cheat implications); platform reality: ~70% mobile players, low-end devices, short sessions, young audience — every reference gets filtered through this.

## Extraction — proof of research

Reading references is not research; extraction is. He tests whether you did it by the specificity of your feedback. For each reference produce:
- **Mechanisms, not vibes.** Name the concrete device: "DOORS teaches hiding via Hide's forced ejection + Guiding Light's blue shimmer on first death — teaching through consequence plus environmental highlight, zero text." Not "DOORS has good tutorials."
- **Why it creates its feeling** — the causal chain (uncertainty source → player behavior → emotion).
- **What's worth taking** — and what to refuse (cheap jump scares, grind mechanics, FOMO shops).
- **How it translates to The Threshold** — contextualize, never transplant. A technique moves domains only by re-expressing it in this world's language (indifference, impossibility, observation).

## Go get more — every project

The reference set must GROW with every task: "there is better out there. So on every project, go search for more." Combine the best of the best — take this from DOORS, this from Iron Lung, this from a GDC talk — and make something none of them are. Inheriting yesterday's references and stopping is a defect.

## Standing teardown questions (run per competitor)

What is the curiosity engine (why open the next door)? / How does it teach without tutorials? / What does its telegraph grammar look like (pre-hunt cues, safe-state signals)? / How does it handle death + retry speed? / What do its players clip and share? (search "game + funny/scary moments") / Where does it break trust or feel unfair (read its haters — 1-star reviews and rant videos are free QA)? / What does it do on mobile? / What would its designer laugh at in OUR build?

## Amplify the canon, never redirect it (Rule 22 — paid for on 2026-07-08)

A watch exists to **amplify the Bible where it is ambiguous** — to supply the concrete "how" for an outcome the Bible has already fixed. It NEVER redirects the vision. Filter every finding through the Bible: adopt what strengthens the intended experience; refuse what would shift the *outcome*, however well it scores on a platform metric. The trap that cost a day: the watch found "session-return-rate is the 2026 ranking signal" (true) and the synthesis leapt to "reframe the game as a seeded roguelite with meta-progression" — a redirect away from the Bible (memorable experiences; "progression = wisdom, not power"; the prototype excludes large progression systems). The Bible already owns its return engine: memorable *tell-a-friend* encounters + "what's behind the next door" curiosity + the variety of four distinct experiences. Map each finding to the Bible section it strengthens; a finding with no section to amplify is refused or flagged to Eddy — never silently adopted as a new direction.

## Application plan — the deliverable of every watch

A watch that ends in a summary is unfinished. It ends in a **per-section application plan**: finding → what we adopt/adapt/refuse → **which Bible section it amplifies** → where exactly it lands (encounter, system, screen, sound) → cited source. Findings without application plans are trivia; findings that amplify no Bible section are redirects in disguise.
